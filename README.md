Solmar Eyewear Dashboard 

The dashboard report has 3 pages each achieving a different purpose.
PAGE 1 -- EDA 
This contains visualisations breaking down the total amount spend vs the revenue generated overall (Blended MER), as well as 
for each type of marketing campaign rolled out, over the entire time frame of the given data. Additionally, there is an interactive 
horizontal bar plot showing the marketing campaigns that had received the maximum amount of investment. Upon clicking on each
individual bar, one will get an estimate of the ratio of total revenue generated vs amount spend on that particular campaign. 
This page also has a stacked area bar chart giving the percentage of revenue generated by new customer and existing ones. One 
can see that the company has been able to maintain an almost perfect balance of revenue from customer acquisition and
repeat customers. 

PAGE 2 -- Growth
This section gives a view of the revenue generated on a particular year with a comparison with that generated around the same time in the past year,
thus giving us a notion of the yearly growth of the company. There was also an attempt at a decomposition tree showing which promotional cycles contributed the most and 
least to revenue

PAGE3 -- Forecast
Contains a forecast to predict future revenue for the period of next 12 weeks.
